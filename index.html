Smart Waste Segregation System

An AI-powered waste sorting prototype that pairs an Arduino UNO with an HC-05 Bluetooth module and a custom Android app built in MIT App Inventor. A Teachable Machine image classifier identifies dry or wet waste through the phone camera, announces the result with text-to-speech, and sends the corresponding command over Bluetooth to the Arduino to trigger the correct sorting action.
